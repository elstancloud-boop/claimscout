Safe by design

People hunting for lost funds are a favorite target for scammers. This is what we never do.

  • We only read public blockchain data.
  • We never ask you to connect a wallet or sign anything.
  • We never ask for a seed phrase or private key.
  • We do not store the addresses you check.
  • Every claim link is checked against a list of official sites.
  • Anyone offering to recover your funds, or asking for your keys, is a scammer.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes.

Which wallets and chains can I check?

Ethereum addresses and ENS names, Polygon (Polymarket) and Solana addresses. More chains, including Arbitrum, Optimism and Base withdrawals, are on the roadmap.

Why might a claim not work?

Projects change their contracts, deadlines and penalties. Always read the official page before you confirm, and expect to pay a network fee.

Do you store my addresses?

No. Addresses are used to read public data and are not logged by the app.

Is the list complete?

No. We cover a small number of sources and add more only after verifying them. A result of "nothing found" does not prove nothing exists.
